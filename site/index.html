Proof history

    How it works

    The controller's readings are cleaned to a fixed set of fields, written in one canonical JSON form and hashed with SHA-256. Only that hash goes on-chain, in a Memo signed by TheCloudPot's wallet.

    Checked in your browser

    This page reads the transaction straight from Solana Devnet and recomputes the hash on your device. It does not trust our servers for either step.

    What it does not prove

    It shows the readings are unchanged since they were anchored and who signed them. It does not show the sensors are calibrated. Grower hardware IDs (MAC addresses) are never published.
